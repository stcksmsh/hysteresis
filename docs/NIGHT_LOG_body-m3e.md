## Design, stated before the first recorded run

Builds on M3d (`m3d*.py` unchanged; new behaviour in `m3e*.py`, `export_cb.py`, `run_m3e.py`). Toy, bar (a)-(c), statistics, tau schedule, chunk (8 blocks), observers' optimiser unchanged.
**Pass/fail in ARGMAX mode** (BRAIN_SPEC 7.19 A2); sampled reported only. Bar for a try = external (a), (b), (c) all pass in argmax. The internal (a) is reported with its own interval and pass flag; "both" is reported, not required for the toy bar.

1. **Closed loop** (`m3e_jax.py`). The cortex still decides once per 0.5 s block (loop rate = block rate; a faster loop is not built). Its input gets 25 numbers at the end of the previous block, taken from the 100 Hz loop: proprioception of all five joints (roll included): position quantised to the encoder step (360/4096 deg) and velocity, delayed one 10 ms control step exactly as `ArmSim` reports it (10 numbers, standardised with the cerebellum's training statistics, clipped +-8); the K1 round-2 cerebellum's predicted position 100 ms ahead (5); its prediction error = realised change of proprioception over the last step minus its +10 ms prediction made one step earlier, in units of the training SD of that change, positions and velocities (10). New input weights start at zero (a test checks the cortex output equals M3d's for any value of the new inputs). The cerebellum is frozen and treated as a sensor: its inputs are stop-gradient. Its GRU runs continuously (state in the carry); the efference ring starts filled with the start pose. Position quantisation passes the gradient straight through (the proprioception inputs carry gradient across blocks within a chunk).
   Cerebellum port: `export_cb.py` (brain env) writes weights + a torch reference on 2 real held-out trajectories; JAX float64 vs torch float32 parity tolerance 2e-4 max abs (mean and logvar, 1,200 continuous steps); test `test_m3e.Cerebellum`. Continuous running vs the 200-step chunks it was trained on, torch MSE vs label (standardised) on the same 12 s: 0.030 / 0.076 / 0.112 (continuous, +10/+50/+100 ms) against 0.095 / 0.138 / 0.158 (chunks restarted from zero). Not checked beyond 12 s continuous; the toy episodes run 60 s (6,000 steps). Report if it matters.
2. **Dopamine** (`m3e.py`). Critic V(x_in) = tanh(x_in W1 + b1) W2 + b2 on the cortex's 41-dim input (32 hidden, own Adam lr 1e-3). Legibility R_b = minus the mean external decoding loss of blocks b..b+3 (2 s) of the training (soft) rollout, for windows fully inside the chunk with all four blocks scored (so up to 5 of the 8 blocks per chunk). delta_b = R_b - V_b (before the critic's update). sigma_delta = sqrt of a running mean of delta^2 (decay 0.9 per chunk). Rate multiplier m = 0.5 + clip(mean|delta| over the chunk's scoreable blocks / sigma_delta, 0, 2), applied to the Adam step of **the program-logit path: cortex output weights `w2[:, :8]` and bias `b2[:8]`**; all other parameters (w1, b1, the other columns, theta) keep the base rate; a chunk with no scoreable window has m = 1. The dopamine lane = delta per block, computed after training on the evaluation rollout with the final external observer and the critic (`dopamine_lane.npy`). Reported: mean |delta|, sigma_delta, share of blocks with |delta|/sigma >= 2 (multiplier clipped at 2.5).
3. **Causal normalisation.** The state y (after M3d's arcsinh on arousal and total surprisal; the M3d global standardisation is gone) and the targets are z-scored with running mean/SD over the past 60 blocks (30 s), the block itself included for its target; the cortex input for block b is the z-score of block b-1 and its change from b-2. Fewer than 8 blocks in the window: z = 0 and not scored. VALID_FROM = EVAL_FROM = 8 blocks (4 s) (M3d trained from block 4). Clip +-5. The arm's loop uses no whole-episode statistic. Descriptor standardisation (observers' inputs, from the calibration pass of the untrained policy, as M3d) and the cerebellum's fixed training statistics are constants, not episode statistics. Tests: a future change leaves the past z-scores and the cortex inputs unchanged; the window is 30 s.
4. **Self-observer.** `observer_int` 37 -> 64 -> 64 -> 8 (tanh) on descriptors of the quantised, delayed positions of all five joints: the 31 body-study descriptors (on the quantised frames, roll excluded as in the body study) plus the roll joint's mean and 90th percentile of |1st|, |2nd|, |3rd difference| (6). Cross-fitted exactly like the external one (groups A, B; refreshed each epoch; 200 steps; last 4 epochs of data). Loss = dec_ext + dec_int + LAMBDA x (mean block energy / E0), energy as a cost (M3d's sign; the order's "- lambda x energy" is read as the energy term of the loss, minimised).
5. **Scoring** (argmax pass/fail): the WO7 observer procedure for EACH decoder separately: a fresh observer (init key 0, 64 hidden), 600 Adam steps, seed 999, trained on 4 hard rollouts (Gumbel keys 100..103, start seed 100) of the 16 training episodes by the scored fixed policy in the scored mode, descriptors standardised by the calibration statistics; evaluation rollout key 7, start seed 4242. (a) = circular-shift gap with 20 seeded shifts >= 4 s, 95 % bootstrap, 10,000 resamples; (b); (c) as M3d. All rollouts are saved to `rollouts/` before scoring.
6. **Lambda grid** on the 8 tune episodes, 12 epochs, argmax mode: lambda in {0.03, 0.3, 3} (M3d's cells), rule fixed now: among cells whose (b) passes, the largest external (a) mean gap; if none passes (b), the largest (b). Then try 1 (40 epochs, lr 1e-3, held-out episodes). Try 2 only if try 1 fails the bar, and then it changes ONLY the policy learning rate 1e-3 -> 3e-3 (stated now). 4 h box each.
7. **Controls for each scored try** (`m3e_controls.py`, WO7 set): (i) untrained (initial weights incl. the zero new weights, kappa as initialised), (ii) trained with kappa forced to 0, (iii) untrained with kappa fixed at the trained per-joint means; the trained policy is rescored in the same call with the same procedure; paired (a)_trained - (a)_control with 10,000 bootstrap resamples, for external and internal separately, both modes.
