# BODY_NOTES: the M0 arm model (scripts/body/arm.xml)

Generated by `make_arm.py` (one source for every number; `arm.xml` is its output). Geometry, axes, ranges and speed/acceleration limits come from
`Rig::illustrative_five_axis()` (`crates/hyst-compile/src/ensemble.rs`) and `body.rs` `LIMITS`. **Everything about the servo and the printed parts is
provisional: no servo has been chosen and no part exists.** These are design numbers, not measurements.

## Servo (provisional): Feetech STS3215, 12 V variant, "30 kg.cm class"

| Number | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Stall torque | 30 kg.cm = 2.942 N.m | 12 V listing, e.g. https://eckstein-shop.de/Motors?mf=1602 and the Seeed ST-3215-C047 datasheet text (30 kg.cm stall, 10 kg.cm rated) |
| Encoder | 12-bit magnetic, 4096 steps/rev (0.0879 deg) | same listings; https://www.mantech.co.za/datasheets/products/sts3215-200620a-1.pdf |
| Gear ratio | 1:345 | same listings |
| Mass | 55 g | Core Electronics / distributor listings of the **7.4 V** variant; the 12 V weight is unconfirmed |
| Body size | 45.2 x 24.7 x 35 mm | from memory of the usual STS3215 outline, **unverified**; only shapes the box inertia |
| Rotor inertia | 1e-7 kg.m2, reflected x345^2 = 0.0119 kg.m2 as joint `armature` | **assumption** (coreless DC motor class), no datasheet number |
| Position gain kp | stall torque / 10 deg = 16.86 N.m/rad | **assumption**: the servo's own loop gains are unknown |
| Damping kv | 2 * 1.5 * sqrt(kp * I_eff), I_eff = mass-matrix diagonal with the arm straight out (4.1, 4.1, 2.4, 1.6, 1.4 N.m.s/rad) | zeta 1.5 chosen by measurement: 0.9 and 1.0-class damping let the shoulder exceed its 300 deg/s2 limit at 50 Hz |
| Friction, backlash, deadband, voltage sag | not modelled | omitted, no basis |

Servo i's case is mounted on the link before joint i (servo 0 on the fixed base); its mass sits at the joint.

## Printed parts (PLA 1240 kg/m3, assumed)

Links are square tubes 20 x 20 mm, 2 mm wall (mass = 1240 * (0.02^2 - 0.016^2) * length); MuJoCo derives each link's inertia from a capsule of that mass
(uniform-density approximation of a hollow part: the real inertia about the joint is a little smaller). Stem: solid 8 mm rod.

| Part | Length | Mass |
|---|---|---|
| column | 0.08 m | 14.3 g |
| upper arm | 0.28 m | 50.0 g |
| forearm | 0.22 m | 39.3 g |
| wrist link | 0.08 m | 14.3 g |
| stem (5 cm stem + 6 cm into the object) | 0.11 m | 6.9 g |

## Mirror-ball implement (rig default `Implement::mirror_ball`, ported to Python in `make_arm.ball()`)

110 tiles; shell: 1.2 mm PLA over a sphere of the mean surface radius 0.0455 m = 38.7 g; tiles: 1 mm acrylic mirror (1180 kg/m3) over the tiles' true areas =
63.2 g. Total 101.9 g, centre of mass taken at the hand point, inertia 2/3 m r^2 about each axis (thin shell) = 1.4e-4 kg.m2. Reach (surface + tile) 0.08 m.
The Rust `radius_m` was not read back; the ported shape gives 0.080 m.

## Totals

Arm 0.448 kg (five servos 275 g, links 125 g, ball 102 g, 1 g base plate). Body masses/inertias are in the compiled model (`mujoco.MjModel.body_mass`, `body_inertia`).

## Dynamics, as built

Timestep 2 ms, implicitfast integrator, 100 Hz control (5 steps). Position actuators clipped at +-2.942 N.m. Bends limited to +-110 deg; base yaw and wrist roll
continuous. Commanded target limited by body.rs `LIMITS` (second-order, braking-aware) in `ArmSim.limit_target`. Proprioception: position quantised to
0.0879 deg, velocity, load (actuator force), all one control step (10 ms) old.

## Numbers from the tests (test_body.py; design checks, no claim about real servos)

- Holding torque at rest, neutral pose: shoulder 1.40, elbow 0.78, wrist 0.25 N.m of 2.94 stall (48 / 26 / 8 %); arm straight out: 1.75 / 0.78 / 0.24 N.m (59 / 27 / 8 %). Sag = torque / kp: shoulder 4.7 deg (neutral), 5.9 deg (straight out); elbow 2.6; wrist 0.8.
- 60 s of full-range random pattern-generator commands: speed <= 0.87 of the limits, acceleration <= 0.91 (50 Hz differences, as `hyst check-csv`); joints inside range.
- MuJoCo CPU vs MJX (JAX on CPU), 10 s trajectory, tolerance fixed before the run at 0.1 deg: max joint difference 0.0001 deg.
- Mean mechanical power sum|tau * omega| over a 60 s check-envelope run: 0.46 W.
- Floor rule (`score_csv.rs`): the column joint sits at z = 0.08 m and the rule wants 0.05 m, so only 30 mm remain there; command envelopes must keep every other point clear.
